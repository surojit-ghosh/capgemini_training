### DevOps

---

#### Waterfall model
- Requirements → Design → Development → Testing → Deployment → Maintenance
- Cant go back to previous stages easily
- this is only suitable for projects with well-defined requirements and low risk of changes

#### Agile model
- Plan → Build → Test → Review → Repeat (Sprint after Sprint)
- Daily stand-up: 15-minute meeting to discuss progress, plans, and blockers
- Sprint: A time-boxed period (usually 2-4 weeks) where a specific
- set of tasks is completed and made ready for review
- Product backlog: A prioritized list of features, enhancements, and bug fixes that need to be addressed in the project
- Sprint backlog: A list of tasks that the team commits to completing during a sprint, derived from the product backlog
- Retrospective: A meeting held at the end of each sprint to reflect on what went well, what didn't, and how to improve in the next sprint
