## Power Skills

#### Dos and Don'ts of PPT
- Decent solid color light background color
- Avoid gradients as background, no white background
- save as 97-2003 ppt format, not pptx
- use light and faded tints of decent pastel colors for background, not bright colors
- Underline: Emphasize and focus on the important points, not the entire sentence
- Font size 18-22
- do not use more than 8 lines in one slide
- italics: casual manner aor relaxed line
- bold: shouting of words
- Font: arial, times new roman, calibri
- if blank add some related image, do not leave it blank
- dont use intro slides
