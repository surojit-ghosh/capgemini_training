### Bubble Sort
- Compares adjacent elements and swaps them if they are in the wrong order.
- Repeats the process until no swaps are needed, indicating that the array is sorted.
- 
### Characteristics
- Time Complexity: O(n^2) in the average and worst cases, O(n) in the best case (when the array is already sorted).
- Space Complexity: O(1) (in-place sorting).
- Stable: Yes (does not change the relative order of equal elements).
